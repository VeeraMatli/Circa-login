## Definition-of-done evidence

Local tests demonstrate email registration, an explicitly MOCK-assured adult profile, preferences, discovery, mutual likes creating one match under concurrent requests, live two-peer WebSocket delivery, retained history after a Worker restart, photo upload/quarantine/local moderation, blocking, actionable report and moderator resolution, entitlement expiry, consumable balance protection, data export and immediate deletion/revocation. None of this is evidence for independent production age assurance, actual abuse detection or live purchases.

Current check results and exact commands are recorded in docs/operations/validation.md. Native build/test, real APNs, StoreKit sandbox, hosted provider delivery, production deploy and capacity scenarios must remain unverified until independently run. A successful Worker dry-run or OpenNext bundle is not a deployed service.

## Required decisions and provisioning

Choose operator/legal identity, launch jurisdictions, age-assurance and moderation/image-processing vendors, support contacts, incident staffing, privacy/retention policy and paid-feature scope. Provision Apple Developer/App Store Connect/signing/APNs and product IDs, Cloudflare account/resources/Access MFA, provider accounts, secret bindings and protected deployment environments. Complete provider contracts and legal review. Keep mock flags disabled and all subscription products disabled in hosted production until the respective flow passes real tests.
