## Privacy
Circa is for adults 18+. We process verified account contact methods, adult-eligibility outcome, private birthdate, profile information and photos, preferences, coarse city location, swipes/matches, messages, safety reports and purchase records to operate the service. Precise identity/contact data is not shown to other members. Optional visibility, read receipts and notification previews are controlled by the user. We do not sell dating-preference data or use photos for unrestricted face identification. Service providers receive only the data needed for their functions under appropriate agreements. Request export or deletion from Profile. Retention may apply to safety evidence, financial records and backups, subject to applicable law. State the actual providers, transfers, lawful bases, data-subject rights and secure support channel before release.

## Terms and community guidelines
Only adults may use Anvaya. Provide truthful information and use photos you have rights to share. Consent is required at every step; a match never creates an obligation to respond or meet. No harassment, threats, hate, scams, impersonation, commercial spam, sexual exploitation, nonconsensual imagery or underage accounts. Report concerns and use Block freely. Serious reports require human review. Moderation decisions may be appealed through the app. Describe enforcement, appeals timeframes, disputes and jurisdiction after legal review. Circa cannot guarantee compatibility, identity authenticity or personal safety.

## Subscription terms
The app displays the price/currency/period from StoreKit. Plans renew automatically through Apple unless canceled within Apple's stated deadline. Manage/cancel in Apple Account subscriptions. Refunds follow Apple and applicable consumer law. Premium never overrides consent or essential safety. No fabricated scarcity, paid age eligibility, fake likes or pressure during conversations. Annual pricing and consumable products are not enabled in this version.

## Safety and support
Choose public meeting places, arrange your transport, share plans only with your consent, and never send money to a stranger. Report fake profiles, spam, scams, harassment, threats, suspected underage use or nonconsensual imagery. Imminent danger requires local emergency services; the app is not an emergency responder. Set up a staffed support website and contact address before launch. Do not imply 24/7 staffing until it exists.

## Account deletion
Profile → Delete my account removes visibility and sessions immediately. Photos, location, contact identities and device registrations are removed, and unreported authored messages are redacted. Legal/safety preservation and backup expiry must be documented and enforced before launch; the current request remains retention_pending rather than claiming full erasure.
